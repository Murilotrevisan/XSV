# Initial trial outlines

Status: planned, not executed. These outlines identify measurements to prepare;
they are not complete procedures or evidence of mission compliance. Acceptance
criteria live in the [mission baseline](../../requirements/missions/INITIAL_MISSIONS.md)
and [budget requirements](../../requirements/BUDGET.md).

Before execution, specify the relevant TBD parameters, configuration/revision,
equipment and measurement method, repeat count, and start/end conditions. Record
water conditions and battery state. Include a retrieval method that does not
invalidate observation of unassisted recovery. Record intervention as such.

| Trial | Requirements | Planned procedure and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TR-001 | MIS-001 | Set the fixed interval and course; position and release the vessel, measure actual traveled distance, and record propulsion interruptions and flotation throughout the run. |
| TR-002 | MIS-002 | Set the buoy layout, navigate outbound and return, record raw time and contact count, and calculate corrected time using the mission formula. |
| TR-003 | MIS-003 | Document ramp and approach geometry, record takeoff and first water impact, measure jump distance, and inspect post-impact function and damage. |
| TR-004 | MIS-004 | Measure and record the selected straight-course length; record timing trigger and finish crossing, elapsed time, and configuration. |
| TR-005 | MIS-005 | Define arena and counterpart configuration, record contact interactions and both vessels' capsize/submersion/recovery events, then inspect XSV function and damage. |
| TR-006 | MIS-006 | Execute TR-001 through TR-004 as one documented sequence; log all battery changes/recharging and any repairs or other replacements. |
| TR-007 | MIS-007 | Test defined capsize orientations and loads; record recovery time, operator input, any manual assistance, and demonstrated navigation after righting. Observe behavior when the link is lost during capsize. |
| TR-008 | MIS-008 | At the defined depth and duration, observe powered onboard operation and propulsion during submersion, link status, ingress, and surface operation afterward. Distinguish a missing radio link from onboard failure; use an observation method that can establish submerged function. |
| TR-009 | CST-001 through CST-004 | Inspect complete BOM quantities/costs, filament mass calculation, bottle valuation, and dedicated-controller inclusion or phone exclusion. Identify estimates and unresolved accounting items before reporting compliance. |
